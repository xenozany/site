PHOBIE d’IMPULSION

PHOBIE d'IMPULSION, COMME UN ENFANT DÉPRIMÉ

01 / 17
read the whole poem
  1. PHOBIE d'IMPULSION, COMME UN ENFANT DÉPRIMÉ
  2. IS THERE MORE NOW THAN WHAT WAS?
  3. WILL I EVER LIVE DOWN WHAT I'VE DONE?
  4. EVEN THOUGH I TRIED TO HOLD BACK FROM JUDGING
  5. I ALWAYS END UP AND REFLECT THAT; NO MATTER HOW MUCH IT LETS ME DOWN,
  6. I CANNOT STOP MYSELF, FROM CARING.
  7. MY MIND IS STUCK AT EMPATHY.
  8. AND FOR WHAT?
  9. FOR SOMEONE WHO WOULDN'T CARE? SUPPORT? OR HELP, ME?
  10. SO CAN YOU TRY TO UNDERSTAND ME?
  11. BECAUSE, THIS IS HOW I SHOW, THAT I CARE
  12. I KNOW NOW, HOW EASY, THINGS TURN AROUND AND BREAK.
  13. HUNDREDS AND HUNDREDS OF MEMORIES GONE WITH A SINGLE MOMENT
  14. ITS SOMETHING I COULD NEVER FORGET
  15. HOW COULD EVERYTHING ,WE'VE DONE.. BE BROKEN?
  16. AND THE WORST PART IS,
  17. I'LL ALWAYS FEEL RESPONSIBLE
